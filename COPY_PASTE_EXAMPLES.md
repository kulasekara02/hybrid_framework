## SLIDE 1: PRESENTATION ISSUES

### Slide Title
**Addressing Presentation Quality Concerns**

### Copy This Table 1: Impact Analysis

| Issue Type | Impact on Interpretability | Impact on Reproducibility | Impact on Scientific Validity |
|------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| Unnumbered Figures/Equations | Readers cannot easily reference specific elements | Prevents precise citation of formulations | Reduces transparency but doesn't invalidate methods |
| Unreferenced Elements | Disconnection between text and visual evidence | Hinders validation studies | Weakens argument structure |
| Low-Resolution Figures | Fine details become unreadable | Prevents accurate data extraction | May hide subtle trends |
| Inverted Figure | Causes confusion, suggests poor quality control | Creates incorrect visual impression | Requires immediate correction |

### Copy This Table 2: Correction Plan

| Step | Implementation | Timeline | Quality Check |
|------|---------------|----------|---------------|
| 1. Figure Numbering | Sequential numbering (Figure 1, 2, 3...) | 2 days | Master list verification |
| 2. Equation Numbering | Consecutive numbering in right margin | 1 day | Alignment consistency check |
| 3. In-Text Referencing | Add explicit references in manuscript | 3 days | ≥1 reference per figure/equation |
| 4. Quality Enhancement | Regenerate at 300+ DPI, vector formats | 2 days | 100% zoom readability test |
| 5. Correct Inverted Figure | Rotate to correct orientation | 1 hour | Peer verification |
| 6. Template Compliance | Apply institutional formatting rules | 1 day | Supervisor review |
| 7. Create Lists | Auto-generate figure/equation tables | 1 hour | Link accuracy check |

**Total Time: 8-10 days**

### Talking Points
"These presentation issues represent quality control oversights that affect readability and navigation, but do not invalidate the underlying scientific methods or findings. I have developed a systematic 7-step correction plan requiring 8-10 days to fully comply with institutional standards."

## SLIDE 2: LATE FUSION JUSTIFICATION

### Slide Title
**Late Fusion Strategy: Justification and Limitations**

### Copy This Table 1: Justifications

| Rationale | Explanation | Supporting Evidence |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Architectural Modularity** | Independent model optimization without constraints | Literature precedent in educational data mining |
| **Interpretability** | Preserves individual prediction outputs | Enables separate analysis of temporal vs. static features |
| **Computational Efficiency** | Parallel training of models | 2 hours vs. 6-8 hours for early fusion |
| **Domain Appropriateness** | Different data modalities (temporal vs. static) | Distinct information sources with different scales |
| **Practical Deployment** | Components updatable independently | Supports integration with existing university systems |

### Copy This Table 2: Limitations & Mitigation

| Limitation | Potential Impact | Mitigation | Future Work |
|------------|-----------------|------------|-------------|
| Suboptimal Interactions | May miss cross-modal feature patterns | RF includes temporal aggregates | Implement early fusion baseline |
| Lack of Comparative Evidence | Cannot prove superiority empirically | Demonstrates effectiveness | Conduct ablation study |
| Overfitting Risk | Meta-learner may overfit | L2 regularization applied | K-fold cross-validation |
| Theoretical Gap | Literature explores early fusion | Focus on interpretability justified | Literature review of fusion strategies |
| Generalizability | Performance bounds unknown | Limitation acknowledged | Encourage replication studies |

### Talking Points
"Late fusion was selected to prioritize model interpretability and practical deployability over potential accuracy gains from early fusion. This represents a methodologically sound trade-off aligned with stakeholder requirements for explainable student support systems. I acknowledge that comparative fusion experiments remain future work to establish definitive performance boundaries."
