## 📋 The Three Questions You'll Answer

### Question 1: Presentation Issues
**Committee's Concern**: "Figures and equations are not numbered, some are hard to read, one is inverted."

**Your Answer**: 
- ✅ Acknowledged the problem
- ✅ Explained impact on interpretability/reproducibility/validity
- ✅ Provided 5-day correction plan with specific steps

**Key Message**: "I understand this affects scientific credibility, and I have a concrete plan to fix it."

---

### Question 2: Late Fusion Methodology
**Committee's Concern**: "You didn't test early or intermediate fusion, so how do you know late fusion is best?"

**Your Answer**:
- ✅ Explained why each fusion type is appropriate or not
- ✅ Justified late fusion based on data structure and interpretability
- ✅ Acknowledged limitation (no comparison experiments)
- ✅ Proposed future work (early fusion baseline)

**Key Message**: "Late fusion is appropriate for this data, but I'll add a baseline comparison to strengthen the claim."

---

### Question 3: Generalizability
**Committee's Concern**: "Your data is from Latvia with international students only. Can this apply to others?"

**Your Answer**:
- ✅ Clearly defined where results apply (similar EU universities)
- ✅ Honestly stated where they don't apply (domestic students, USA, etc.)
- ✅ Outlined validation requirements (OULAD, cross-institutional data)
- ✅ Committed to documenting limitations in thesis

**Key Message**: "Results are specific to this context, but I have a plan for external validation and will clearly document limitations."
