## 🎓 Key Defense Strategy Points

### 1. Acknowledge Limitations Proactively
✓ "This represents a limitation of the current study scope"
✓ "Future research should empirically validate..."
✓ "The findings are most directly applicable to..."

### 2. Show You Have a Plan
- Presentation issues: 7-step correction plan with timeline
- Late fusion: Future work includes comparative fusion experiments
- Generalizability: Clear validation roadmap with sample sizes

### 3. Emphasize Core Contributions
Even with limitations, your thesis contributes:
1. Novel hybrid LSTM + Random Forest architecture
2. First systematic cultural factor integration
3. Explainability framework with root causes
4. Early warning system (week 8-12)

### 4. Demonstrate Scientific Maturity
- Don't be defensive
- Be transparent about limitations
- Frame limitations as future research opportunities
- Show you understand the broader scientific context

## 📊 Table Formatting Tips

### For PowerPoint/Google Slides:
1. **Use simple table layouts** - 3-4 columns maximum per table
2. **Font size**: Minimum 14pt for readability
3. **Colors**: Use your institutional colors or neutral professional palette
4. **Alignment**: Left-align text, center-align headers
5. **White space**: Don't overcrowd - better to split across slides

### For LaTeX Beamer:
```latex
\begin{frame}{Presentation Issues - Impact Analysis}
\begin{table}
\small
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{3cm}|p{3cm}|p{3cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Issue} & \textbf{Interpretability} & \textbf{Reproducibility} & \textbf{Validity} \\
\hline
Unnumbered & Cannot reference... & Prevents citation... & Reduces transparency... \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
\end{frame}
```
