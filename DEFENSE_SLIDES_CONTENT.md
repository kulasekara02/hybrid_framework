## Slide: Question 2 - Late Fusion Justification

### Why late fusion without comparing early/intermediate fusion?

| **Fusion Type** | **When Used** | **Why Not Used Here** |
|----------------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Early Fusion** | Single model for all features | ❌ LSTM needs 3D temporal data, RF needs 2D static data |
| **Intermediate** | Deep learning only | ❌ Random Forest has no "middle layers" to fuse |
| **Late Fusion** ✓ | Different model types | ✅ **Respects data structure** + **Interpretable** |

### **Advantages of Our Choice**:
- LSTM specialized for time sequences → 55% weight
- Random Forest specialized for static features → 45% weight  
- Clear contribution tracking for explainability

### **Acknowledged Limitation**:
No empirical comparison with alternatives

### **Future Work**:
Add early fusion baseline to demonstrate late fusion superiority (2 days)

## Slide: Question 3 - Generalizability Limits

### Can results apply to other student populations?

| **Context** | **Current Study** | **Generalizability** |
|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 🌍 **Geography** | Latvia (EU) | ⚠️ Limited: Different systems in USA, Asia |
| 👥 **Population** | International students only | ⚠️ Limited: Domestic students have different needs |
| 🏫 **Institution** | 5 small universities | ✓ Moderate: Similar EU institutions |
| 📅 **Duration** | 32-week calendar | ✓ Good: Standard semester length |

### **Where Results Apply**:
✅ Small-medium European universities with international students  
✅ Similar cultural diversity (South Asia, Africa)  
✅ 30-36 week academic calendars

### **Where Results DON'T Apply**:
❌ Domestic students (no cultural adaptation factors)  
❌ Large USA universities (different system)  
❌ Online-only programs (no campus integration)

### **Required Validation**:

| **Action** | **Timeline** |
|-----------|-------------|
| Test on OULAD dataset (UK, 32K students) | 1-2 months |
| Cross-institutional data (2-3 universities) | 6-12 months |
| Document limitations clearly in thesis | 1 week |

### **Commitment**: Add "Generalizability Limitations" chapter to thesis
