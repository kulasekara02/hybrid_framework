## 🔬 WHY HYBRID IS BETTER (Scientific Reasons)

### Reason 1: Complementary Strengths
**LSTM Good At:**
- Temporal sequences
- Time-series patterns
- Engagement trends over weeks

**Random Forest Good At:**
- Static relationships
- Feature importance ranking
- Non-linear interactions

**Together:** They cover different aspects → Better overall prediction!

### Reason 2: Reduced Overfitting
- Single deep model = Can overfit easily
- My hybrid = Two different model types check each other
- Meta-learner learns optimal weighting
- **Result: More robust predictions!**

### Reason 3: Domain-Appropriate
Student success depends on:
- ✅ **Temporal factors** (engagement patterns) → LSTM captures this
- ✅ **Static factors** (demographics, culture) → Random Forest captures this
- Hybrid naturally fits the problem structure!
