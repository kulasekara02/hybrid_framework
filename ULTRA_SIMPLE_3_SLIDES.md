## QUESTION 2: Why Late Fusion?

**Committee Question:**  
"You combined LSTM and Random Forest using 'late fusion'. But you didn't test 'early fusion'. Why is your choice good enough?"

---

## MY ANSWER:

### Why I Chose Late Fusion:

**5 Good Reasons:**

1. **📊 Easy to Understand**  
   Teachers and advisors can see how each model works separately

2. **⚡ Faster Training**  
   Takes 2 hours, not 6-8 hours

3. **🔧 Easy to Update**  
   Can improve one part without changing everything

4. **📚 Proven Method**  
   Most educational research uses this approach

5. **🎯 Right for My Data**  
   Student data has two different types (engagement + demographics)

---

### What I Admit:

**Honest Limitations:**

| What's Missing | What This Means | My Response |
|----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Didn't test early fusion | Can't prove late fusion is "best" | It's "good enough" for thesis goals |
| Might miss some patterns | Early fusion could find more connections | Added some aggregate features to help |
| No comparison study | Less scientific rigor | Will recommend for future research |

**My Position:**  
✅ Late fusion is **appropriate** for my study  
✅ It achieves my research goals  
⚠️ Testing other methods is important **future work**
